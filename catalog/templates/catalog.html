The System

Versatility, Precision, and Seamless Integration.

Versatility, Precision, and Seamless Integration

The Osteo-Fit CMF System is engineered to provide surgeons with a versatile and reliable fixation solution through an extensive range of plate designs and thickness options.

Optimized Surgical Workflow — The system features Micro, Mid, and Mini configurations, meticulously organized within a single compact tray to streamline the surgical process. Our Free-bending L-type plates are available in 1mm increments for precise anatomical contouring, while the vertically adjustable boss plates facilitate rapid and accurate fixation, particularly essential during complex Le Fort procedures.

Superior Handling & Stability — The system offers both standard and self-drilling screws (available in 1.2mm, 1.6mm, 2.0mm, and 2.4mm diameters). The self-drilling design eliminates the need for pre-drilling, significantly reducing operative time. Enhanced engagement between the screw and driver shaft ensures exceptional "pick-up" performance and stability, providing surgeons with a confident and user-friendly operating experience.
